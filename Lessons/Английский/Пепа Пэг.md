A lot has happened in my life: I was shot at, I fell in a puddle, and lay there for a long time. Suddenly, we needed to watch the cartoon: Peppa Pig. I think the cartoon has two main ideas. The first is that jumping in puddles is cool. The second is probably that it's cool to get to know other people's cultures.
However, something crazy happened in the cartoon. At first, everyone arrived at the train station—nothing special. The characters safely reached the palace and met the Queen, but then things took a turn for the worse. The Queen, taking advantage of her status, simply confiscated the bus from the driver—the guy, by the way, was about to get fired! After that, the characters reached Big Ben, where Edmond the elephant pompously showed off his encyclopedic knowledge. Then the Queen brought everyone to a bridge, which turned out to be closed because a ship was sailing on the river at the time. However, disregarding the children's safety, the Queen decided on a desperate maneuver and jumped the bus over the rising part of the bridge, where they got stuck—and only miraculously no one fell into the water. Finally, they arrived at a square, where it suddenly started raining, and everyone decided to jump in puddles—true degenerates.
I do not recommend this cartoon. The adult character intentionally breaks the rules, putting children in danger. The child will eventually copy the destructive behavior.
When I was a kid, my favorite cartoon was probably Transformers Prime; it was a really cool cartoon  with a serious plot. Now I'm more into anime, so my two favorites are JoJo's Bizarre Adventure and Don't Tease Me, Nagatoro.
